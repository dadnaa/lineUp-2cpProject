# Line Up — Job Search & Recruitment Platform

**Line Up** is a multidisciplinary project  for my second year at ESI SBA  designed to simplify and improve the recruitment process by connecting job seekers with employers through an intuitive, structured platform.

Developed over four months, the project aims to make job searching more accessible and recruitment more efficient by bringing job listings, applications, CV management, and recruitment decisions together in one place.

## Features

### 👤 For Job Seekers

- **Browse Job Offers:** Explore available job opportunities without creating an account.
- **Apply for Jobs:** Submit applications directly through the platform by uploading a CV and authenticating your account.
- **Explore Companies:** View detailed company profiles to learn more about potential employers.
- **Manage Profile:** Maintain and update personal profile information.

### 🏢 For Employers

- **Publish Job Offers:** Create and manage job listings to attract potential candidates.
- **Review Applications:** Access submitted applications and review candidates' CVs.
- **Manage Recruitment:** Evaluate applicants and accept or decline applications.
- **Manage Job Listings:** Organize recruitment opportunities through the platform.

### 🔐 Authentication & Application Management

Line Up integrates authentication into workflows that require an account, allowing job seekers to submit applications and employers to manage recruitment activities through their respective interfaces.

## Project Goals

- Simplify the job search and recruitment process.
- Provide a user-friendly experience for both job seekers and employers.
- Improve access to job opportunities and company information.
- Centralize job listings, applications, and recruitment decisions.
- Create a structured connection between candidates and recruiters.
